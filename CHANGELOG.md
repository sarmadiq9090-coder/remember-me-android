# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

First build of Remember Me for Android, for the owner only.

- Reminders with title, note, time, repeat (daily, weekly, chosen weekdays, every N days, every N
  hours, monthly, yearly), early notice, priority and category, in the Windows app's data format.
- Exact alarms (alarm clock) re-armed after reboot, update, clock and time-zone changes.
- A due reminder stays as a notification with «تم» and «أجّل» until dealt with, plus a full-screen
  reminder screen with Done, Skip and six snooze choices.
- Overdue, Today, Upcoming and Done tabs; search; a 30-day trash; a home-screen widget; a "New
  reminder" shortcut.
- «السماح بالعمل في الخلفية» screen with checks for notifications, exact alarms, full-screen, battery,
  "pause if unused", Do Not Disturb and the phone maker's settings.
- Export and import as a zip that Remember Me for Windows restores; safety copies before an import.
- Arabic by default, English in Settings; Western digits; light and dark themes.
- No internet permission.
