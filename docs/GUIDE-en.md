# Remember Me for Android 1.0.0: user guide

Remember Me reminds you of appointments and tasks on time. All your data stays on your phone: no account, no
statistics. It goes online only to check for and download updates from GitHub, and sends no reminder or data
about you (Settings → Updates to turn it off).

## 1. First start
- The app asks for notifications, then opens Reminder health. Allow everything marked "Needed" so every
  reminder arrives on time.
- If a red bar says "Reminders may not arrive on time", tap Fix.

## 2. Adding a reminder
- Tap New reminder at the bottom and type one line, e.g. "remind me to call mum in an hour" or "meeting
  tomorrow at 9 am".
- New reminder opens a full-screen window: × to close and Save at the top, the field, then the rows Date and
  time, Category, Priority, From a template and More options. Tap Date and time (the clock) and pick the date,
  then the time, without typing them. It starts at
  today and the next full hour. A picked time stays even if the text names another, and the × next to it removes
  it. A time that has passed offers "Tomorrow, same time".
- A preview of the time the app understood is always shown. When the time is unclear the app asks ("9 am or
  9 pm?") and does not save until you choose.
- More options opens the full editor: time, repeat, up to 3 early alerts, category and tags, priority, note and
  steps, and Alert options.
- From a template: birthday, monthly bill, medical appointment, daily medicine and more. A template only fills
  the fields; nothing is saved before Save.
- Also: share text from any app to Remember Me, long-press the app icon (Quick add, Today), add the New reminder
  tile to Quick Settings, or use the home-screen widget.

## 3. When it is time
- A pinned notification with Done, Snooze 10 minutes and Snooze until…; the full-screen reminder may appear over
  the lock screen.
- On the reminder screen, Done is at the end and Snooze next to it, with more snooze choices above. Taps are
  ignored for the first second so you don't press by accident.
- A volume key silences the ringing without closing the reminder.
- After Done from the notification, "Done — Undo" stays for 10 seconds.
- A reminder that arrives more than 5 minutes late (for example, the phone was off) says so.

## 4. Alert type and tone
- Normal reminder: follows the phone. Silent = no sound and no vibration; vibrate = vibration only.
- Important alarm: rings at alarm volume even when the phone is silent, for 30 s, 1 or 2 minutes.
- Remember Me **never overrides Do Not Disturb**. To let important alarms ring during it, allow alarms in the
  phone's settings (Settings → Reminder health → "Important alarms and Do Not Disturb" explains how).
- Tone: the phone's tone, 3 Remember Me tones, an audio file of yours (copied into the app, 5 MB at most), or
  Silent (vibrate only). If a tone is missing the next one is used: a reminder is never silent by accident.
- Full screen over the lock screen is for important alerts only («Important alarm» or importance «High»); a
  normal reminder is a pop-up notification with its sound. The Full screen switch turns it off for those too;
  there is no option for every reminder. Lock screen: show or hide details ("You have a
  reminder" with Done and Snooze).

## 5. Home
- Sections: Overdue, Today, Upcoming, and Done (folded).
- Swipe a reminder one way for Done with Undo, the other way for snooze choices (can be turned off).
- Long-press to select several: Delete, Priority, Move, Snooze, Done.
- View in the top bar: List, Agenda, Day, Week, Month.
- Search finds title, note, tags, category and state, ignoring Arabic letter variants and diacritics.

## 6. Lists and tags
- The three categories (Personal, Work, Study) are shared with Remember Me for Windows.
- A list is a saved tag. With two or more lists, their chips appear at the top of Home.

## 7. Prayer times and the Hijri date
- A reminder can follow a prayer ("15 minutes after Asr"). You choose the city and the method; no location
  permission. Times are always approximate.
- The Hijri date (Umm al-Qura) is turned on in Settings → Display, with a ±2 day adjustment; a reminder can repeat
  every Hijri year.

## 8. Reminder health and the alert test
- Settings → Reminder health: everything a reminder needs, where a block comes from (Android or the phone maker),
  and one button per problem. Anything that cannot be read shows "Unknown", never "Ready".
- Test the alert now: choose the type and when (in 10 seconds, in an hour, tomorrow 7:00). Six parts are checked;
  no reminder is saved.
- Compatibility report: a short text about the phone and its settings only, shown in full before you share it.

## 9. Your data
- Save a copy: a file Remember Me for Windows opens too; it tells you first what Windows does not support.
- Encrypt the copy with a password (Windows cannot open it; a lost password cannot be recovered).
- Weekly automatic copy to a folder you choose (the newest 5 are kept).
- Restore from a file: a summary first; your current data goes to safety copies before anything is replaced.
- Import and export calendar files (.ics). Delete all data asks you to type the confirmation word first.

## 10. If a reminder is late
1. Open Reminder health and follow every Fix.
2. Run Test the alert now with "tomorrow 7:00" and leave the phone overnight.
3. Some makers (Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, OPPO, vivo) stop background apps: follow their steps under Phone maker.
4. The alert log shows when each alert arrived and how late, without any title.

## 11. More features
- **Voice input**: the microphone in Quick Add. **Privacy note:** voice input goes through your phone's speech
  service (usually Google's), which may work over the internet; we ask it to work offline, on the phone itself,
  when it can. Remember Me itself has no microphone permission and sends neither your voice nor your reminders anywhere. A note says so on first use and in
  About. Without a speech service the microphone is hidden. If the service fails or you close it, a message shows
  and the cursor returns to the field with the keyboard: use the keyboard's microphone. Before opening Google's dialog
  the app asks the service which languages it supports and picks from them: the phone's, then Arabic (Saudi
  Arabia), (Egypt), (UAE), then Arabic; without an answer it starts with Arabic (Saudi Arabia). After a failure
  the next try uses the next language.
- **Read aloud**: per reminder in Alert options, or in Settings. Never in silent mode or Do Not Disturb; on a
  locked phone with "hide details" it only says "You have a reminder".
- **Morning summary**: Settings → Morning summary → choose the time. A quiet notice with today's count, none
  when there is nothing.
- **Change history**: Settings → Organisation (off by default). It keeps when a reminder was snoozed or moved,
  without titles, on your phone only; cleared from Settings; opened from the editor's ⋮ menu.
- **Complete the reminder when all steps are done**: Settings → Organisation (off by default).
- **What's new**: shown once after updating to 1.0.0; from it you can turn every important reminder back to a
  normal one.
- **Privacy**: Settings → About → Privacy: every permission the app asks for, and why.

## 12. Updates and reports (1.0.5)
- **Updates**: Settings → About Remember Me → Check for updates. It also checks by itself at most once every 7 days
  when the app is opened. An update is installed only after checking that it is newer, that its SHA-256 matches the
  published one and that it is signed with Remember Me's own key, and then only when you confirm on Android's
  screen. Settings → Updates: "Check for updates automatically" and "Download over Wi-Fi only" (both on). If you
  leave the app during the download, "Update ready to install" shows when you come back.
- **Report a problem or suggestion** (Settings or About): write what happened, then "Send by e-mail" or "On
  GitHub". Your mail app or browser opens with the text, and you send it. "Attach device information" is optional
  (version, Android, phone, language only); no reminder is ever attached.
